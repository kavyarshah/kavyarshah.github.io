Hi!

I work across research, policy, and strategy on questions related to science, technology, innovation, and institutions.

I am interested in how complex social systems behave, how technologies diffuse through them, and why interventions produce different outcomes in different contexts. My work brings together disparate sources of data and evidence to better understand these dynamics, and I am particularly interested in computational and network-based approaches to studying them.

Much of my work has focused on health, scientific capacity, and emerging technologies in developing and emerging economies.

I currently work at the United Nations Development Programme India Office.
