Practice

I work with public institutions, philanthropies, and nonprofits to decide where to invest, which partners to bring together, and how to design programs that can work in practice. I synthesize evidence and map the institutions already working on a problem so those decisions reflect local priorities and capacity.

Science infrastructure

Scientific capacity and open science

MetaDocencia

The initiative aims to expand research capacity and strengthen public trust in science across Latin America. I’m working with MetaDocencia on the design and fundraising strategy for a multi-country program that explores how AI can support researchers and advance open science.

Portfolio to Protect Science

Freedman Consulting

The portfolio focused on keeping critical U.S. scientific data available and usable over time. I led the investment strategy, helping philanthropic funders identify where their support could safeguard the infrastructure that researchers rely on.

Technology policy

AI and national security mapping

Freedman Consulting

The engagement gave philanthropic funders a clearer view of where additional support could make a difference in AI governance and national security. I mapped the organizations and initiatives already working in the field to identify gaps and inform funding priorities.

Development

AI and the Sustainable Development Goals

UNDP India

The goal is for public institutions in India to adopt AI inclusively and use it to advance the Sustainable Development Goals. I lead work on UNDP India’s national strategy and state-level programming, connecting national priorities with opportunities for implementation in individual states.

AI and health in Uruguay

MetaDocencia

Identifying where AI could address health priorities in Uruguay is the focus of this work with MetaDocencia.

Public-private partnerships for rural health

Himalayan Health Initiative

The goal is to make primary, maternal, and preventive care more accessible in rural Uttarakhand by connecting community-led services with the public health system. I advise on how to structure those partnerships and develop an operating model that can sustain local care over time.
