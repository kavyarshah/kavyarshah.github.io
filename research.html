Regenerative Transitions Lab

How can institutions shape markets to steer innovation toward more sustainable outcomes? With Rajesh Veeraraghavan and Parijat Chakrabarti, we're comparing case studies to understand how interventions shift the direction of innovation and how the conditions for success vary across contexts. To support that comparison, I’m developing an LLM-assisted research methodology and a standardized taxonomy for producing structured, reproducible case studies of market-shaping interventions.

Mapping Public-Sector AI Initiatives in Africa: Deployment, Funding, and Institutional Networks

What shapes where public-sector AI is deployed across Africa and which problems it is used to address? This project examines how funding and relationships among governments, funders, and implementing organizations have shaped patterns of deployment from 2015 to 2026.

Indian pharma and the nation's global health diplomacy

Drawing on expert interviews and policy analysis, my thesis examined how the commercial priorities of India’s pharmaceutical industry shaped the country’s global health diplomacy, from its advocacy for affordable medicines to its evolving stance on intellectual property. Read the related article.
