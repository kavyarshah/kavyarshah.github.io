Work in progress

Regenerative Transitions Lab / Symbiocene Initiative

With Rajesh Veeraraghavan and Parijat Chakrabarti, I designed an LLM-based tool and a shared classification system to make case studies of market-shaping interventions easier to compare and reproduce. I’m supporting a comparative analysis of more than 60 cases to understand how these interventions direct innovation toward sustainable outcomes.

Mapping Public-Sector AI Initiatives in Africa: Deployment, Funding, and Institutional Networks

I’m building a database of public-sector AI initiatives across Africa from 2015 to 2026 to map where projects are deployed, how they are funded, and how governments, funders, and implementing organizations work together.

Projects

Disease X

I’m interested in how epidemiological models can inform preparedness for a novel disease when early evidence about transmission is limited. My work uses visualizations to help researchers, policymakers, and practitioners interpret findings, understand uncertainty, and discuss response options.

Indian pharmaceuticals and global health

My Georgetown thesis examined how India’s pharmaceutical industry both enables and constrains the country’s influence in global health. I used expert interviews and policy analysis to trace how the industry’s commercial priorities shape India’s health diplomacy. Read the related article.
